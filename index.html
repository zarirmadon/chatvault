How do I get a backup?
ChatGPT: Settings, Data controls, Export data. You get an emailed zip.
Claude: Settings, Privacy, Export data. You get an emailed zip.
If the option is missing on a phone, switch the browser to Desktop site.

Newer backup later? Tap Add or update and pick it. New chats are added, changed chats are replaced by the newer copy, and you get a count of each.
